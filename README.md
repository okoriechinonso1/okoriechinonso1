# Chinonso Okorie

**Ph.D. Candidate in Industrial Engineering** · FAMU-FSU College of Engineering, Florida A&M University

I build mathematical optimization models, custom algorithms and machine learning models for public transportation, shared mobility, supply chain & logistics, and airline operations. I work in the Transportation System Optimization (TSO) Lab advised by Professor Yanshuo Sun.

**Website:** [okoriechinonso1.github.io](https://okoriechinonso1.github.io)

## Research

- **Electric-truck routing** for municipal solid waste collection: solutions within 2% of optimal, up to 90% faster than a commercial solver
- **Charging infrastructure planning** with bi-level, multi-period optimization models
- **Service districting** for ADA paratransit: balanced zones in under 7 minutes instead of up to 3 hours
